Optional cookies and advertising measurement

With your permission, this page uses the Meta Pixel to measure collection visits, product views, checkout starts and approved purchases. The Meta Conversions API also lets our server report purchases whose approval has been verified with Mercado Pago. Both mechanisms use the same technical purchase identifier to prevent duplicates. Events may include the product, amount in Brazilian reais (BRL), page address, campaign parameters, cookie identifiers and technical browser and connection information. Meta may connect this information with its services for measurement and advertising.

Meta code and the advertising context sent to our server require a fresh choice for this version of measurement; earlier permission is not automatically reused. With this permission, the server may use the IP address and browser identifier (User-Agent) received with the payment request, plus the identifiers of the _fbp and _fbc cookies when they exist. We do not invent click identifiers. The API receives the page address without query parameters or fragments.

Declining does not prevent browsing, buying or receiving your files. You can change your choice at any time through , also available in the footer. When you withdraw permission, we stop new browser events, remove Meta measurement first-party cookies accessible to this site and ask the server to cancel pending transmissions linked to that permission. If the connection fails, the browser keeps only the random consent identifier and date for up to 30 days to retry the request on a later visit or reconnection. An active browser Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal keeps this measurement off.

We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, Brazilian tax IDs, card numbers or security codes as event parameters, and we do not use automatic advanced matching. Purchase events use a technical identifier derived from the transaction without sending the raw order number. Your preference and its random identifier may be kept in the browser for up to 180 days; a fresh opt-in creates a new identifier. Local event markers help prevent duplicates, and purchase markers expire within 180 days or are removed when you decline measurement.

Advertising matching data on the server (IP address, User-Agent and cookie identifiers) is deleted after a confirmed transmission or after the server receives a revocation; if transmission remains pending, it is kept for no more than 30 days. Technical markers used to prevent duplicates and record revocations, without that matching data, may be kept for up to 180 days. These periods do not change the necessary retention of payment and delivery records.

This optional advertising processing depends on your consent. Meta may process information internationally under its Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy. Withdrawing permission does not automatically erase information Meta has already received. For requests about that information, use the contact channels on this page and in the provider policy.
