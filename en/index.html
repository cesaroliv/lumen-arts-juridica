What do I receive with the complete collection?

Ten individual ZIP downloads, each containing a 16,000-pixel-high PNG master, its applicable license and instructions. The artworks were upscaled using AI.

Are the artworks printed or framed?

No. This purchase includes digital files only. Interiors, table frames, wall frames and styled room scenes shown on the page are illustrative—not items supplied.

Can I print at large dimensions?

The masters are 16,000 pixels high. Physical results depend on size, paper, printer, color management and finish. For large prints, ask your print shop for a proof before final production.

How do payment and downloads work?

Payments are processed by Mercado Pago or PayPal, according to the option available at checkout. Once approval is confirmed, protected download links for your digital files appear in the order confirmation.

Can I pay from another country?

Prices and payments are in Brazilian reais (BRL) in Brazil, and US dollars (USD) elsewhere, with international payments through PayPal. The currency and amount are shown before you confirm. Availability depends on your country, account and payment provider.
